About

The writer

I'm Somoye Okikijesu Boluwatife. I write serialized fiction rooted in a Nigerian voice — currently running a twelve-part story, A Beginning, Not a Game (also published as Rent Free), on InkNaija. Alongside the fiction, I take on content writing, copywriting, and correspondence work. I studied English Language at Tai Solarin University of Education, and I'm comfortable adapting tone and register across formats — from a brand's blog post to a character's dialogue.
