About

I'm Somoye Okikijesu. I'm a Nigerian writer interested in fiction, character, and human relationships.

I write serialized fiction and short stories, with a particular interest in character-driven narratives. My stories often explore relationships, family, ambition, identity, and the choices people make when their lives become complicated.
